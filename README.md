Hi 👋 My name is Smriti Gurung

Web, App Developer and Designer
-------------------------------

I'm a passionate Web & App Developer and Designer who enjoys turning ideas into practical, user-friendly digital products. I work across both frontend and backend development, with experience building mobile apps, web applications, APIs, dashboards, and complete business solutions.

I enjoy working with technologies like Laravel, Flutter, React, PostgreSQL, and modern UI/UX tools, while constantly exploring better ways to design, build, and deploy software.

Alongside development, I also occasionally work on UI/UX design and graphic design, creating clean interfaces, visual concepts, and digital assets that complement the products I build.

Beyond coding and design, I'm interested in product development, startups, and solving real-world problems through technology. I love taking projects from an initial idea to a working product and continuously improving them through experimentation and learning.

Currently focused on building meaningful products, sharpening my full-stack skills, improving my design thinking, and creating software that people actually find useful.
